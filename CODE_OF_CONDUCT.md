# Code of Conduct

GitGrader is a community of educators, students, and engineers. We expect
everyone participating in our projects to help keep it a welcoming place.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and constructive; critique code and ideas, never people
- Assume good intent, and be patient with newcomers and non-native speakers
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or trolling — anywhere in
  issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, or community channels
- Respect maintainers' time and decisions; disagreement is fine, hostility is not

This code of conduct is aligned with the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported privately to the maintainers at
**conduct@git-grader.org** or through a direct message to any maintainer.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban participants who violate these rules.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all repositories in the `git-grader`
organization and to all official community spaces.
